MacMinion

A native Mac addon manager for The Elder Scrolls Online. Find, install, update, back up, and remove ESOUI addons, without the Java Minion app.

Download for Apple silicon Install guide

macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon. Free for personal use. No account, no server of its own: MacMinion reads your AddOns folder, matches it to the public ESOUI catalog, and downloads addons when you ask.

What it does

OverviewStatus, stats, update history, libraries your addons need, and suggestions. Installed & Find MoreA card grid or list of your addons and the whole ESOUI catalog, with filters and ratings. UpdatesUpdate one addon or all of them, pin a version, and keep MacMinion itself current. BackupsSavedVariables, settings, and your addon list, in Minion's own format. AIChangelog summaries, suggestions, and plain-words search, with Ollama on your Mac or Claude. Command linemacminion for scripts and AI agents, running through the app.

Start here

  1. Install MacMinion and open it once with right-click > Open.
  2. If you used Minion, MacMinion imports its install records and update history on first launch. See Coming from Minion.
  3. Check Settings > General points at your AddOns folder (live, PTS, or any folder).
  4. Press ⌘U to update everything, or browse Find More.

In the app, Help > MacMinion Help (⌘?) opens these pages, and the ? buttons open the page for what's on screen.
