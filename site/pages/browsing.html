Installed & Find More

Your addons, and everything on ESOUI you don't have yet. Both show as a card grid or a list.

Installed

MacMinion matches each folder in AddOns to an ESOUI entry. Each card shows the installed version and its status:

StatusMeaning
Up to dateThe installed version is ESOUI's latest.
Update availableESOUI has a newer version. Update is on the card.
IgnoredUpdates are skipped for this addon (pinned, or you chose Ignore Updates).
Version unknownInstalled outside MacMinion and Minion, so the version isn't recorded. Reinstall it once to track it.

Not on ESOUI lists folders no ESOUI entry claims: addons you made or copied by hand. MacMinion leaves them alone, but you can remove them.

Find More

The whole ESOUI catalog, minus what you have installed. Search by name or author in the toolbar. Select a card to read its details, description, and changelog in the inspector, then Install.

Filters

Thumbs up and down

Every card, row menu, and details pane has 👍 and 👎. Ratings steer the AI: a thumbs up means "more like this", and a thumbs down keeps that addon out of suggestions and AI search for good.

Install a specific version

In an addon's details, the Version menu lists every release ESOUI still keeps. Choosing an older one installs it and pins it: Update All skips it until you install the latest again. ESOUI publishes no checksum for archived releases, so MacMinion checks only that the zip unpacks into addon folders.

Remove

The trash button on a card (or Remove… in its menu) asks first, then moves the addon's folders to the Trash. Its SavedVariables stay, so reinstalling keeps your settings.

Grid or list

Switch with the toolbar control, ⌥⌘1 (grid) and ⌥⌘2 (list). Card size, preview images, and grouping are in Settings > Layout.
