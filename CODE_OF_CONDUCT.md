# Code of conduct

MacMinion follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), in issues, pull requests, and anywhere else people talk about the project.

In short: be kind and welcoming, accept feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the people who use MacMinion. Harassment, insults, and personal attacks are not accepted.

## Reporting

Email **macminion@solaegis.com** to report unacceptable behavior. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept private. The maintainer may remove comments, lock threads, or block people who break this code of conduct.
